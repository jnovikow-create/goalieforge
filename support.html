How to use it

  • Pick a level: Development (U9–U12), U13–U15, U16 AAA, Junior / College, or Professional. Enter body mass so fuel targets can scale.
  • Plan shows that level’s week, sleep target, and cautions. Tap a day to open the session.
  • The session timer runs each drill. Save an effort score (RPE) at the end.
  • Recovery is a short check-in for sleep, soreness, hip or groin pain, and energy. Repeated hip or groin pain above 3/10 shows a warning to ease off and see a clinician.
  • Fuel builds a game-day timeline from puck drop, plus a tournament plan when games are close together.
  • Progress charts recent training load. More holds the drill library and the research links.

What this app is

GoalieForge is educational guidance for off-ice goalie training. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a dietitian’s plan. Hip pain, concussion symptoms, or an eating concern need a physician, physiotherapist, or registered dietitian.

Common questions

Where is my log?
On this iPhone. Deleting the app removes it. A device backup can restore it if GoalieForge was included.

Is there an account?
No. There are no ads and no sign-in.

Something broken?
Email jnovikow@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing.
